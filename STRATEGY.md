## 5. The production system (full-time, 5–6 days) — your core question

You have a studio for high-finish A-roll. Good. The winning model is **not** "film something new every day." It's **record once, post ten times.**

### 5a. The weekly engine

```
MON  ── PLAN     Run the topic engine (this repo) → pick 1 long-form + ~12 short hooks
TUE  ── RECORD   Batch day in studio: 1 long-form (20–35 min) + 6–10 short hooks back-to-back
WED  ── BUILD    Capture screen-recordings (the actual product/terminal/Telegram) for b-roll
THU  ── CUT      Long-form edited (Descript) + OpusClip auto-slices it into 6–10 verticals
FRI  ── SHIP     Publish long-form (YT) + schedule the week's shorts (IG/TikTok/Shorts)
SAT  ── ENGAGE   Reply to every comment/DM, post 3–5 stories, X build-thread, Reddit value post
SUN  ── off (or a 30-min metrics review: sends-per-reach + activation rate)
```

### 5b. Short-form: how many, and what they look like

- **Volume:** **2 posts/day on IG** (≈14/week), each **cross-posted to TikTok + YouTube Shorts** (so ~6 platform-posts/day for ~5 min of extra work). Research sweet spot is 3–5 quality Reels/week; you beat that *because most are slices of one long-form recording*, not 14 separate shoots. Quality bar still applies — see 5e.
- **Length:** **15–30s** for new-audience reach (completion rate), **30–90s** for watch-time/storytelling. Optimize for **retention %**, not a fixed length. A 12s clip held to 85% beats a 60s held to 30%.
- **Anatomy of every short (this is "what they look like"):**

| Time | Element | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5s | **Visual pattern interrupt** | Bold on-screen text + a striking first frame (a number, a logo, the result). No slow intros. |
| 0–3s | **Spoken hook, 7–12 words** | "Stop paying $100/mo for Claude Code." / "This free repo replaces a $24k tool." 50% of people leave in 3s — earn the 4th second. |
| 3–25s | **One idea, one demo** | Show the thing *working* (screen-record). One insight, not five. Cut every 1.5–3s, kill dead air. |
| last 3s | **CTA for SHARES, not just comments** | "Send this to someone wasting money on AI tools." + "Comment HERMES for the free guide." Captions ON the whole time (80% watch muted). |

- **The CTA upgrade that breaks your plateau:** you currently end with "comment X." Keep it (it feeds AutoDM + leads), but **add a send trigger**: *"Send this to the friend who's still paying for ChatGPT."* Sends-per-reach is weighted 3–5× likes and is the #1 driver of *new* reach. This one change is the difference between 530k and 5M.

### 5c. Long-form: how many, and what they look like

- **Volume:** **1/week minimum** (Nate Herk runs 2 — work up to it). Batch-record 4–6 in a single studio day every few weeks (same shirt, same set — your own doc's "treat it like shipping software" instinct is right).
- **Three tiers** (from your doc, kept):
  - **HERO (1/mo):** 20–35 min, SEO title, designed to rank for years. *"Free AI Agent Setup 2026", "I Built a Free AI Trading Bot", "I Quit Claude Code — Here's What I Use".*
  - **USE-CASE (2/mo):** 8–15 min real builds. Doubles as **creator bait** — others replicate and credit you.
  - **EDUCATION (1/mo):** 12–20 min "how AI works" — feeds your Skool modules.
- **Anatomy of a hero tutorial:**
  1. **0–30s cold-open payoff:** show the finished result first (the bot trading, the agent texting you) — *then* "here's how to build it free."
  2. **The build, narrated:** screen-record, leave *some* errors in (authenticity = trust, and it's your noob-friendly superpower).
  3. **The payoff + recap:** it works; here's what you built.
  4. **CTA ladder:** free signup → myhermes.cloud → "full template/community in the description (Skool)."
- **Titles that rank** (your doc's list, validated by Herk's actual titles): *"[Expensive Tool] Alternative That's Free" · "I Built [Result] in [Time] (No Code)" · "Stop Paying for [Tool]" · "Complete [Topic] Setup 2026" · "I Quit [Tool]. Here's Why."* Add a **specific dollar outcome** when you can — that's Herk's highest-performing pattern.
- **Thumbnails:** your face (intense, not smiling) + 3–4 word phrase + high-contrast bg. One Canva template, swap text/photo. Make it once.

### 5d. Editing intensity (what "high finish" actually means here)

- **Captions always on**, burned-in, styled. Non-negotiable.
- **A cut/visual change every 1.5–3s.** Speed-ramp pauses, jump-cut filler words.
- **Zoom punch-ins** on the screen-recording when you point at something.
- **Tools:** **Descript** (edit long-form like a doc, auto-remove "ums"), **CapCut** (free; auto-captions, silence removal, templates), **OpusClip** (auto-slice long-form → vertical clips with captions; cuts repurposing from ~4 hrs to ~30 min). You do **not** need Premiere to look professional.

### 5e. The quality bar (when NOT to post)

Post only if the answer is yes to all three: **(1)** Does the first 3s make someone stop? **(2)** Is there one clear, *useful* takeaway? **(3)** Would someone *send* it to a friend? If a clip is off-pattern (your 0–30 run reels), it's better unposted — off-pattern volume trains the algorithm to show you to the wrong people.

## 8. Learning the material (you're starting from basics — here's how to teach credibly)

This is the part you're least confident about. It's also the most fixable. You do **not** need to be an expert. You need to be **one chapter ahead** and honest about it.

### 8a. The "one chapter ahead" method
Pick the build for next week. Learn *just* that. Build it (struggling is fine — record it). Teach the exact steps. Your audience is *behind* you, not ahead — being two weeks into a topic makes you the perfect teacher for someone zero weeks in. This is exactly how Nate Herk started.

### 8b. The six concepts — explained so well you can teach them on camera
Learning the analogy *is* learning the concept, and each analogy is also a 60-second Reel (Script D / education tier). Memorize these:

| Concept | The 1-sentence analogy you say on camera |
|---|---|
| **API** | "A waiter: your app tells the waiter what it wants, the kitchen (another service) makes it, the waiter brings it back. You never enter the kitchen." |
| **MCP** | "USB-C for AI: one standard plug that lets your AI use any tool — your files, GitHub, a database — without custom wiring each time." |
| **SDK** | "A furniture kit with the right tools in the box: pre-built code so you don't carve every screw yourself." |
| **HTTP** | "The postal system of the web: requests are letters, responses come back, status codes (200/404) are 'delivered' / 'address not found'." |
| **SQLite** | "A spreadsheet in a single file that your app can query instantly — a database with no server to run." |
| **Vector database** | "A librarian who shelves ideas by *meaning*, not alphabet — so 'how do I cancel' finds 'refund policy' even with no shared words. It's how AI 'remembers' and searches." |

Get these six right and you can hold your own with 95% of your audience. When you hit the edge of your knowledge on camera: **"I had to look this up too — here's what I found."** That honesty *builds* trust; it doesn't lose it.

### 8c. Your build-to-learn loop (your products are the curriculum)
You already own two teaching machines: **Hermes** (the agent) and **content-bot** (your automation repo). Every module in your Skool course and every long-form video should be *a real thing you build with them*. You learn by building the content you're going to publish. The work and the learning are the same hours.

### 8d. Sounding professional without overclaiming
- **Script the open, wing the middle.** A tight first 10 seconds = professional. Edit out the stumbles (Descript). Polished ≠ expert; polished = *edited*.
- **Show, don't assert.** "Watch this run" beats "trust me, this is powerful."
- **Use real numbers** (yours are great: "2,094 visits in 15 days," "93.75% retention," "$0 to run"). Specifics read as credible; adjectives read as hype.

### 8e. Weekly learning ritual (2–3 hrs, Sunday/Monday)
Read 1–2 of the GitHub repos you'll feature + the relevant Anthropic/tool docs, build one small thing, write the analogy you'll use. That's it. The topic engine (this repo) hands you the trending repos so you're never hunting.

## 9. Fix the leak: myhermes.cloud activation (your real path to 10k users)

This section is worth more than the rest combined, because it's where your growth currently dies.

**The data again:** 4,428 visitors → 106 waitlist signups → **18 instances started** → 13 paid. The product retains 93.75% once people are in. So the entire problem is the **visit → running-instance** chasm.

**Ranked fixes (do them in order):**

1. **Kill or shrink the waitlist.** Your own AutoDM notes say "temporary waitlist… lifted in a day or two." Every day it's on, you're converting demand into a dead-end. A waitlist on a free product is a growth tax you can't afford at this stage. If you need to throttle for cost, gate by *usage* (free credits) not by *access*.
2. **Cut time-to-first-value to under 2 minutes.** 76% bounce + 1-min sessions = people don't get to value fast enough. The magic moment is the *first Telegram reply from their agent*. Get them there before asking for an API key if at all possible (offer a "try it now" sandboxed chat — you already have `try_chat_opened` / `free_message_sent` events, so the surface exists; push everyone through it first).
3. **Add UTMs to every link — today.** Your doc flagged this as *critical* and it's still unfixed: **zero UTM attribution on 42 AutoDM campaigns.** You currently cannot tell which reel drove which signup. The `scripts/utm.py` tool in this repo fixes it in minutes (see §12). Format: `myhermes.cloud?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=reel_<ID>&utm_medium=autodm`.
4. **Instrument the activation funnel as a saved PostHog funnel** (`landing_viewed → free_message_sent → instance_started → subscription_activated`) and watch it weekly. You have all these events already.
5. **Reduce the API-key wall.** Requiring an OpenRouter/Anthropic key before *any* value is your steepest cliff. Offer a small amount of hosted free credits so the first session needs zero setup; ask for their key when they hit the limit (`landing_quota_exhausted` already fires — trigger the upgrade nudge there).

> If you 10× traffic (your 5M-views goal) *without* fixing this, you get ~180 instances and ~130 paid/mo. If you fix activation to a modest 10% visitor→instance and 5× traffic, you get ~2,000+ instances/mo. **Activation is the multiplier, not views.**
