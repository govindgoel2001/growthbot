## 0. Reality check on "30k in a few months"

It's possible — but only one way. The honest version, from real comparables (0→30k in ~6 months is "fast but real" for a focused niche at 2+ long-form/week; **Shorts drive 30–50% of new-sub growth**):

- **Long-form ranks and compounds** (your moat for *years*), but it's slow to start.
- **Shorts are the accelerant** that can do 100k–1M+ views off one good hook, and that's what makes "a few months" achievable.
- You already have an **8k IG audience + a working DM funnel** — that's a launch boost most channels don't have. Use it to seed every upload in week 1.
- **It is gated by 1–2 breakout videos.** No one hits 30k fast on a flat line of 2k-view videos. The plan below maximizes the number of "at-bats" (volume) on *proven-demand* topics (your AutoDM winners) so a breakout is likely, not lucky.

**The 14-week roadmap (aggressive but real):**

| Week | Long-form live | Shorts live | Sub milestone | What's driving it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 3 | 14 | **300–800** | IG cross-promo seeds first views; Shorts start testing hooks |
| 3–4 | 4 | 14 | **2,000** | First long-form starts ranking; 1 Short pops |
| 5–6 | 4 | 14 | **5,000** | Trading-bot hero lands (your 1,420 topic); Shorts compounding |
| 7–8 | 4 | 14 | **10,000** | A breakout Short (100k+); search traffic builds |
| 9–10 | 4 | 14 | **16,000** | Playlists bingeable; returning viewers |
| 11–12 | 4 | 14 | **23,000** | 2nd hero ranks; collabs/shoutouts |
| 13–14 | 4 | 14 | **30,000** | Flywheel: Shorts → long-form → subs |

If by week 6 you're under ~3k, you're not behind on effort — you're behind on **hooks/thumbnails**. Fix those before adding volume (see §8, §10).

## 6. The Shorts engine (your accelerant to 30k)

Shorts are 30–50% of your sub growth. The math to 30k basically *requires* a couple of Shorts to pop. System:

1. **Cut 5–7 Shorts from every long-form** with OpusClip (4 hrs → 30 min). The "moments" are pre-marked in the shot-lists above (the hook, the reveal, the error-fix, the price line).
2. **Each Short is self-contained:** 0–2s pattern interrupt + on-screen text, a 7–12 word hook, one payoff, end with "full build on my channel."
3. **Captions burned in, cut every 1.5–3s**, vertical, face/screen in frame.
4. **Title the Short with the search keyword** + a number/result. First line of text = the hook.
5. **Cross-post the same Short to IG Reels + TikTok** (rewrite caption). One cut, three platforms.
6. **Daily posting** — the algorithm rewards volume on Shorts more forgivingly than long-form.
7. **Every Short ends with "subscribe / full build on the channel"** — Shorts→long-form→sub is the flywheel.

Target: **7 Shorts/week minimum.** Over 14 weeks that's ~100 at-bats. You need ~2 to break out.

- **Tags + hashtags:** 3 hashtags in the description (#AIAgents #FreeAI #Automation).
- **End screen:** subscribe + "next video" (point to the logical next in the playlist).
- **Playlists:** add every video to its playlist immediately — playlists drive binge watch-time.
- **First 24 hours:** reply to every comment, post the video to IG stories + the free Skool community, and pin a comment with a question (drives comment velocity).

## 10. The 5 numbers to watch (and the fix if each is low)

| Metric | Healthy early | If it's low, fix… |
|---|---|---|
| **CTR (thumbnail)** | 4–8%+ | the thumbnail + title (not the content) |
| **Avg % viewed** | 30%+ long, 70%+ Shorts | the first 30s / the hook + pacing (more cuts) |
| **Subs per video** | climbing weekly | the CTA + giving a reason to subscribe ("I build these weekly") |
| **Shorts that break 50k** | ≥1 per 2 weeks | the first 2 seconds + the hook line |
| **Video → signup (PostHog)** | rising | the CTA ladder + the description link (UTM) |

**The one rule:** if a video underperforms, 9 times out of 10 it's the **thumbnail/hook**, not the topic. Re-test the packaging before you abandon a proven topic.
